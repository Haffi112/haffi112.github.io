Each dot is a unit on a drifting graph. It fires once r of its neighbours have fired at it within a short window, then rests. The slate squares are inhibitory: each of their spikes cancels half of an excitatory one. A cascade either fizzles or sweeps the graph. This is the model from my PhD work on bootstrap percolation with inhibition.
